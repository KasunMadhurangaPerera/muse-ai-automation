Privacy Policy

Little Stories Studio

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Little Stories Studio operates the Muse AI Video Automation project, a workflow being developed to help authorized users create, review, and publish short-form video content.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, the application may process:

  • Basic connected account information, such as account identifiers and usernames.
  • Authorization credentials required to access connected services.
  • Videos, titles, captions, and other content selected for publishing.
  • Publishing statuses, identifiers, and error information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to identify authorized accounts, prepare approved content, initiate publishing requests, verify outcomes, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Third-Party Services

The workflow may use external automation, hosting, video-generation, and social media services, including TikTok, when connected and authorized. Such services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Account Authorization

Connected accounts are authorized through the relevant service's supported authentication process. Requested permissions should be limited to features needed for the integration.

6. Data Storage and Retention

The project is currently in development. Storage locations, retention periods, and deletion procedures must be confirmed before production use. Authorized users should avoid connecting sensitive accounts until those practices are documented.

7. Data Sharing

Information may be transmitted to connected services when required to provide requested functionality, including uploading videos to authorized social media accounts.

8. User Choices

Users may revoke an application's access through the relevant connected platform's account settings. Revocation may prevent future publishing operations.

9. Security

The application is under development. Security controls and credential handling must be verified before production deployment.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features, data practices, or service integrations change. Changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the application operator using the contact information provided on the official website.
